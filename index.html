Questions

Good to know

Is Voltra really free?

Yes. The free version is a full working app: draw diagrams and plans, run the checks, and print or export to PDF. It's limited to 5 circuits and one floor-plan page and adds a watermark to exports. Pro removes those limits.

Which systems does it run on?

Windows 10/11, macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Linux. It's a standard desktop app — if your current software runs on your PC, Voltra will too.

Where is my data stored?

On your own computer. Voltra is a desktop app; your drawings and customer details are saved as files on your machine and are never uploaded. Only a subscription, if you buy one, involves our server (just your e-mail and plan).

Can I open files from other software?

Yes — Voltra imports single-line files from other Belgian tools, including Trikker .trik files, so you don't have to redraw your existing work. Voltra is independent and not affiliated with Trikker.

Why does my computer warn me on first launch?

Because the app isn't code-signed yet. It's safe to run — see the note under Download — and you can verify the file against its published SHA-256 checksum.
